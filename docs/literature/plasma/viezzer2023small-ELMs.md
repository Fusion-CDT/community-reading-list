Excellent review paper of a number of small and no-ELM regimes. Covers I-mode, QH-mode, EDA H-mode, and small ELM regimes such as QCE and grassy ELM H-modes.

There is a mention of active supression techniques (RMPs, vertical kicks, impurity seeding, etc.), useful for a literature search.
