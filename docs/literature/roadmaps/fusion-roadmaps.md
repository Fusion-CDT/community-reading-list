National strategies and roadmaps that set out where fusion research is heading, and why. Useful for seeing how your own research fits into the bigger picture, and for the introductions of reports and theses.

- **UK Fusion Materials Roadmap 2.0** (UKAEA, supported by the Henry Royce Institute, 2025): an update to the 2021 roadmap, reflecting progress since then and covering a wider range of materials challenges.
- **UK Fusion Materials Roadmap 2021–2040** (UKAEA and the Henry Royce Institute, 2021): the original roadmap, developed with over a hundred materials experts, setting out the main areas of work needed to make materials for fusion power plants such as STEP.
- **UK Fusion Strategy** (UK government, March 2026): the government's plan for growing the UK fusion industry, including £2.5 billion of research funding over the five years to 2030.
- **Fusion Science & Technology Roadmap** (US Department of Energy, October 2025): the US strategy for getting fusion power onto the grid by the mid-2030s, including the science, materials and technology gaps that still need closing, such as breeding and handling fusion fuel.
- **Energy and emissions projections** (UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, annual): projections of UK energy demand, greenhouse gas emissions and electricity generation to 2050. Useful context on the energy system fusion would join.
